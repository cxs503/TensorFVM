## 2. Method

- Momentum: explicit staggered-MAC advection (first-order upwind) with central diffusion; wall-adjacent faces use the half-cell ghost (`u_ghost = -u`), so no-slip is second order at the wall.
- Projection, `masked` arm: solid cells are removed from the pressure system; every face of a solid cell carries a hard zero normal velocity, i.e. homogeneous Neumann for the correction; the outlet plane is closed with `phi = 0` outside, which makes the operator SPD and enforces `Q_out = Q_in`. The matrix is factorised once (SuperLU, MMD ordering) and reused for every step.
- Projection, `legacy` arm: the historical Brinkman treatment -- unmasked whole-domain Poisson solve, momentum penalty sink, then a post-projection relaxation `u <- u/(1 + kappa dt)` on faces touching the body. That last operation is applied *after* continuity has been enforced, so it is the candidate root cause.
- Mass ledger per step: `max|int_cell div u|/(U H)` over fluid and over solid cells, the net flux through the immersed surface `int_dS u.n dS` (accumulated as the spurious volume drift), the gross flux, the inlet/outlet imbalance and `max|u|` inside the body.
