# `native_sections3d`: exact planar sections, verified

Repo entry: `src/tensorfvm/native_sections3d.py` (`cell_section_polygons`).
Evidence: `results.json`, `table.md`, `section-rings.png`, `ring-deficit.png` (all
regenerated by `python3 scripts/audit_native_sections.py`, which exits non-zero if any
published claim fails). Tests: `tests/test_native_sections3d_exact.py` (9 cases).

## 1. The verification debt that was paid

Before this audit **no test in `tests/` referenced `native_sections3d`** (0 of 217 test
modules), while the module is the geometry behind every native section figure in
`docs/verification-*` (channel SST/DNS, hybrid RANS/scalar, compatible inertia,
gradient jump, pressure diagnostics). Its docstring makes four checkable claims:

1. sections are **stitched** from the actual facet/plane intersections (no convex hull);
2. coplanar facet edges **cancel** their internal triangulation diagonals;
3. zero-area **tangencies are omitted**;
4. a broken finite-area ring **raises** instead of being replaced.

A defect here is silent: the figure still renders and the report still ships.

## 2. Method — anchors are analytic and independent of the module

| case | anchor |
|---|---|
| box `[0,1]^3`, plane `z = 0.5` | closed form area `1` (all grid refinements) |
| graded box (non-uniform `x, y, z` spacing) | same closed form, `1` |
| plane on a boundary face / outside the mesh | face area `1` / empty set |
| O-grid annulus `R = 1, r = 0.5` | closed form `(n/2) sin(2pi/n)(R^2 - r^2)` for the **same** discretisation, i.e. zero truncation left; continuum limit `pi(R^2-r^2)` with the pure quadrature deficit `sin(t)/t - 1`, `t = 2pi/n` |
| single-vertex tangency (tetra, `z = 1`) | no polygon, no exception; the `z = 0` face gives `1/2` |
| convex-hull negative control | a hull of the same ring omits the hole and overestimates by `R^2/(R^2-r^2)` = `+33.33%` at `r = R/2` |

The annulus anchor carries no truncation error of its own, so the tolerances in the
tests are machine-precision tolerances (`1e-13`) rather than plausibility bands, and
the refinement case is **predictive** (measured deficit vs the analytic `sin(t)/t`).

## 3. Results (numbers injected by `scripts/audit_native_sections.py`)

See `table.md`; headline values from `results.json`:

- box sections: deduplicated area error `<= 1e-15` for `n = 1, 2, 4, 8`, polygons stay
  quadrilaterals (no diagonal triangles left by the coplanar rule);
- ring sections: relative error vs the closed form `<= 1e-15`; the measured deficit
  tracks `sin(t)/t - 1` to `<= 1e-15` and quarters when `n_theta` is quadrupled
  (observed ratio ~16, O(h^2));
- hull control: the measured area is the ring area, `+33.3%` below the hull value, so
  claim (1) is falsified-for-a-hull and holds for the shipped stitching;
- tangency: dropped (0 polygons, no exception); boundary-coincident plane keeps the
  genuine face.

## 4. Finding worth knowing downstream: coincident planes are double-sided

When the section plane *coincides* with an interior grid plane, both owners of that
face emit their own (identical) ring. So for `n = 2, 4, 8` at `z = 0.5` the polygon
count is `2 * nx * ny` and `sum(polygon_area)` is exactly **2x** the geometric section
area (measured ratio `2.0` to 12 digits). This is the right behaviour for per-cell
colouring (each cell gets its own polygon) but it is wrong for anyone quoting a
"section area".

Two consequences, both now pinned by tests:

- `sum(polygon_area)` is **not** the section area on a coincident plane; use
  `tensorfvm.verification.native_sections.unique_section_area`, which deduplicates
  identical rings and reproduces the closed form to `1e-15`;
- a downstream metric that divides by the section area to get e.g. a mean velocity was
  silently off by a factor 2 whenever its sampling plane landed on a grid plane.

Non-coincident planes (the normal case for the shipped figures, e.g. `z/h = 0.047`) are
single-sided and summed area equals the geometric area.

## 5. Error judgement

Key quantities and their error against the analytic anchor: box unique area `<= 1e-15`,
ring area vs closed form `<= 1e-15`, ring deficit vs analytic `<= 1e-15`, tangency
area `0` exactly. **All are far below 3%**; the binding limits are floating-point
round-off, not discretisation. Not covered here (honest scope statement): only
axis-aligned planes are supported by the reviewed API, so tilted-plane sections and
their intersection-segment ordering are outside this verification; facet-plane
intersections producing more than two hits raise by design and were not exercised
beyond the tangency cases.
