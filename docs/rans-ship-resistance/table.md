# RANS (k-omega SST and Spalart-Allmaras) ship-resistance verification

Generated by `scripts/run_rans_ship_resistance.py`. Do not edit by hand.

## 0. Case, method and toolchain

**Physical problem.** A tanker hull at Froude numbers `0.046 <= Fn <= 0.142` (5-15.5 kn full scale, model scale 1:45.714). Total resistance decomposes as `C_T = (1+k)C_F + C_W`; this work computes the viscous branch `(1+k)C_F` with two RANS closures and quantifies what is and is not delivered for the wave branch.

**Toolchain.** `tensorfvm.rans_ship_resistance` (new): a thin-shear-layer marching RANS that solves the streamwise momentum equation together with the full k-omega SST (Menter 2003) and Spalart-Allmaras (1994) transport equations, on 2-D, axisymmetric and wake domains, with a fixed exponential wall-normal grid, backward Euler in `x`, implicit tridiagonal wall-normal diffusion, Newton linearisation of the quadratic sinks and Patankar source limiting. KVLCC2 is represented by a parametric hull whose `Cb`, `Cm`, `Cwp`, `LCB` and wetted surface are verified before any resistance number is produced, and by its equal-sectional-area equivalent body of revolution, whose surface speed comes from the axisymmetric source distribution.

**Why this rung of the ladder.** The repository already owns a 1-D equilibrium channel matrix (`docs/rans-turbulence-validation-matrix`, SST/k-omega/k-epsilon, no SA) and a 3-D double-body form-factor attempt that is machine-recorded as WITHHELD (`docs/resistance-chain-form-factor`: ~3e6 cells, ~63 h of setup, native SST diverging at every affordable spacing). The marching RANS is the affordable rung in between: it keeps the full closure and the wall-normal transport and drops only the streamwise elliptic coupling.

## 6. Notes, limitations, and the error judgement

**Where the numbers do and do not support a claim.**

* The laminar branch reproduces Blasius in the same solver: profile, skin friction, `delta*`, `theta` and `H` all within a few percent, which is what makes the turbulent branch credible -- the turbulence model is the only difference between the two runs.
* Both closures land within a few percent of the Prandtl-Schlichting line at `Re = 1e7`; they sit 7-8 % above the 1/7 power law, which is itself the cruder anchor (the two anchors differ by 4 % at this Re). The criterion is therefore recorded pass/fail against *each* anchor separately rather than against whichever one happens to agree.
* Mass conservation is satisfied to round-off because the wall-normal velocity is obtained from the discrete continuity equation; this is a check against coding errors (a missing metric factor in the axisymmetric case shows up immediately) and it is reported as such, not as a physical prediction. The von Karman momentum identity is the *independent* momentum check: it compares `C_f/2` with `dtheta/dx + (H+2)theta/U_e dU_e/dx + theta/R dR/dx` built from three different discretisations.
* The Reynolds-number scaling of `C_F` on the hull is weak by construction (5 to 15 kn changes `Re_L` by a factor 3 and `C_F` by 10 %), so a `C_T = a + b Fn^2 + c Fn^4` fit through the *viscous* branch is a fit to the residual Reynolds dependence, not to wave making. The fit is reported with its `R^2` and is explicitly not offered as a Froude scaling law of total resistance.
* Spalart-Allmaras admits the laminar state as an exact fixed point. In this marching framework it is stable and matches the flat plate, but two of the seven hull speeds fell onto the near-laminar branch, which destroys the SA Froude fit. This is reported as an honest failure of the SA branch on the hull, with the mechanism named (trivial fixed point of the model combined with a stiff destruction linearisation), not smoothed over.
* Wave resistance is **not computed**. A Michell thin-ship implementation ships in the module and is *falsified* by its own Froude-scaling invariant (a hull doubled in linear dimensions at `U -> sqrt(2)U` must keep `C_W`; the implementation returns exactly twice the value), so it is used in no verdict. The wave term is replaced by the residual the SIMMAN anchor would imply, and the anchor's own provenance is withheld (no primary workshop table in the workspace, no network route), so criterion C3 is withheld regardless of the numbers.

**Does the key quantity meet 3 %?**

* `C_F` flat plate vs Prandtl-Schlichting: 2-3 % -- inside 3 % (SST slightly above, SA below).
* Von Karman momentum identity: below 1 % -- inside 3 %.
* `C_V` at `Fn = 0.142` vs the SIMMAN anchor: 1-3 % -- inside 3 % *but* not interpretable as a total-resistance validation, because the wave term is missing and the anchor is unverified; the honest statement is that the viscous branch is consistent with the anchor only if the residual resistance is close to zero, which for a tanker at `Fn = 0.142` it is not.
* Hull wetted surface vs the published value: about 5 % -- outside 3 %, and stated as the accuracy limit of the parametric hull, which is the reason the resistance coefficients are reported with that geometry caveat attached.
