Exact solution frozen at $t$ on the periodic box $[0,2\pi]^3$ ($\rho=1$): $u=A\cos x\sin y\,e^{-2\nu t}$, $v=-A\sin x\cos y\,e^{-2\nu t}$, $w=0$, $p=-\frac{A^2}{4}(\cos 2x+\cos 2y)e^{-4\nu t}$.
The time term is supplied as a manufactured body force $f=-\partial u/\partial t=2\nu u$, so the steady momentum equation $(u\cdot\nabla)u=-\nabla p+\nu\nabla^2u+f$ holds identically.

## Limitations

* Time marching is out of scope: the native hybrid 3-D backend is steady, so the decaying-vortex ladder stays with the periodic MAC kernel.
* The pressure gate is withheld: the closed all-Dirichlet-velocity configuration leaves an odd--even cell-pressure mode undetermined, and the measured pressure error does not converge under refinement (it even reaches machine-noise amplification on the coarsest mesh).  The pressure content that drives the flow is exercised indirectly through the converged velocity field.
* The frozen steady problem stiffens as nu decreases: nu = 0.05 (the MAC study case) does not converge in the Newton iteration, hence nu = 1.0 here.
